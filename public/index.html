Building footprints for Mumbai, each carrying a roof height and an inferred use. Colour, filter and click anything to read its attributes.

Footprints and heights come from GlobalBuildingAtlas's open building data and from Overture Maps. Use is inferred: from OSM tenants and nearby anchors where they exist, and from a size-and-height heuristic where they don't. of buildings fall back to that heuristic, so read the typology as a first pass rather than a survey — colour by typology rule to see which buildings were actually evidenced.
